ERP Implementation Roadmap

A structured functional and technical blueprint for deploying cloud enterprise resource planning systems, focusing on core financial ledgers, bank reconciliation, AP workflow automation, and custom field adjustments.

Phase-by-Phase Implementation Lifecycle

01 Discovery

Requirements & Scope

Audit legacy ledgers, map business workflows, and establish baseline chart of accounts.

02 Architecture

Design & Configuration

Configure core financial segments, AP workflows, and vendor management rules.

03 Execution

Data Migration & Testing

Execute multi-entity test runs, validation checks, and OCR invoice capture tests.

04 Go-Live

Deployment & Training

Enforce role-based access, complete bank reconciliation handoffs, and release user access.

Business Central & Sage Intacct: Functional & Technical Expertise

📊 Core Financials

General Ledger & Multi-Entity Setup

  • Structuring custom chart of accounts, dimensions, and global segments.
  • Configuring multi-entity company books and consolidated reporting.
  • Managing fiscal periods, journal entries, and trial balance audits.
📑 AP & Purchasing

Accounts Payable & Vendor Processing

  • End-to-end vendor bill creation, management, and approval routing.
  • Three-way matching for purchase orders, item receipts, and bills.
  • Vendor payment terms, discounts, and aging report analysis.
💳 Cash Management

Bank Reconciliation & Clearing

  • Importing and reconciling bank statements with general ledger transactions.
  • Handling clearing accounts, deposit slips, and payment adjustments.
  • Resolving discrepancy logs and maintaining cash flow visibility.
⚙️ Customizations

Customizations & Currency Conversions

  • Using XML, SQL, and JavaScript for tailored field customizations.
  • Implementing basic currency conversion logic across transactions.
  • Adapting form layouts and data fields to fit business requirements.
⚡ Document Automation

OCR & PaperSave Workflow Automation

  • Deploying PaperSave and OCR tools for automated invoice data capture.
  • Mapping scanned line items directly to Business Central and Sage bills.
  • Configuring automated multi-tier approval rules and audit trails.
🛒 Procurement

Purchasing Modules & Inventory Flow

  • Setting up purchase requisition workflows and automatic PO generation.
  • Managing inventory receipt tracking and vendor item pricing matrices.
  • Streamlining the complete order-to-payment lifecycle.
